Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my selfie?

On the home screen, tap "Retake Selfie" at the bottom. You can pick from your photo library or take a new one.

How do I change my name or grade?

Tap "Change Profile" on the home screen. This clears your progress and lets you start over with new info.

Can I play more than 30 minutes?

The daily cap is 30 minutes. When you run out, you can earn up to 10 extra minutes per day by answering 5 bonus questions correctly. After that, come back tomorrow — a full 30 minutes resets at midnight.

My raccoon lost my face!

Tap "Retake Selfie" and pick a clearer photo of your face. The app works best with photos where your face is centered and well-lit.

Why is the app asking me to rotate my phone?

The game plays in landscape mode. The menus work in either direction, but once you start a level, rotating your phone sideways gives you the full playing field.

Do you collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug!

Email madhokr@gmail.com with a description and, if possible, a screenshot. We'll get to it.
