# Iron Horizon Support

Iron Horizon is a real-time strategy game for iPad. Ten campaign missions, an unrestricted Absolute War mode, and 26 combined-arms units across land, sea and air.

## Contact

For bugs, feedback, or anything else, email madhokr@gmail.com. Please include your iPad model and iPadOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## Controls

Tap a unit to select it. Drag a box with one finger to select a group. With units selected, tap the destination or an enemy to move or attack. Long-press the ground to attack-move. Use the All Army button at the bottom left to select every fighting unit. Long-press a group slot numbered 1, 2 or 3 to save the current selection, and tap it to recall them. Pan the camera with a two-finger drag and pinch to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump across the map. Tap a factory then tap the map to set its rally point. Leave a battle with the close button at the top left.

## Frequently asked

How do I win? Hold the territory target shown at the top of the screen, or destroy the enemy Command HQ. In Absolute War you can also seize the centre objective and hold it for thirty seconds.

Why will my Refinery not place? Refineries must be built on an oil field you own, so capture the square first.

Why will my Naval Yard not place? It must sit on your ground touching water.

Where did my campaign go? Progress is saved per Game Center account, so it follows the signed-in player.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the privacy policy for details; there is nothing to collect.
