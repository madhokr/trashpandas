# Iron Horizon — Support

**Iron Horizon** is a real-time strategy game for iPad. Ten campaign missions, an unrestricted Absolute War mode, and 26 combined-arms units across land, sea and air.

## Contact

Bugs, feedback, balance complaints, or anything else: [madhokr@gmail.com](mailto:madhokr@gmail.com)

Please include your iPad model and iPadOS version if you're reporting a problem.

## Quick reference — controls

- **Select a unit** — tap it
- - **Select a group** — drag a box with one finger
  - - **Move / attack** — with units selected, tap the destination or an enemy
    - - **Attack-move** — long-press the destination
      - - **Select every fighting unit** — the All Army button, bottom left
        - - **Save a control group** — long-press a group slot (1, 2, 3)
          - - **Recall a control group** — tap that slot
            - - **Pan the camera** — two-finger drag; **Zoom** — pinch
              - - **Jump on the map** — tap the minimap
                - - **Set a factory rally point** — tap the factory, then tap the map
                  - - **Leave the battle** — the X top-left, then confirm
                   
                    - ## Frequently asked
                   
                    - **How do I win?** Hold the territory target shown at the top of the screen, or destroy the enemy's 2x2 Command HQ. In Absolute War you can also seize the centre objective and hold it for 30 seconds.
                   
                    - **Why won't my Refinery place?** Refineries must be built on an oil field you own. Capture the square first.
                   
                    - **Why won't my Naval Yard place?** It must sit on your ground touching water.
                   
                    - **Where did my campaign go?** Progress is saved per Game Center account, so it follows the signed-in player.
                   
                    - **Does the game collect my data?** No. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html) — there's nothing to collect.
