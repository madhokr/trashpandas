# Iron Horizon — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

Thank you for playing **Iron Horizon** ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how. In short: **the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us, any server, or any third party.**

## What the App stores

Iron Horizon saves your campaign progress — completed missions, star ratings, best times, and totals — to a file inside the App's own private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device and is not readable by other apps. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

## Game Center

The App offers optional sign-in to **Game Center**, a service operated by Apple, not by us. If you sign in:

- Your campaign save file is labelled with your Game Center player identifier so that a different player signing in on the same iPad gets their own separate campaign. That identifier is stored only on your device.
- If Game Center leaderboards or achievements are enabled, your star total and achievement progress are sent to **Apple's** Game Center service, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Sign-in is entirely optional. Declining it does not limit any gameplay; the App simply plays offline and saves locally.

## What the App does not do

- No advertising identifiers, no ad networks
- No analytics or telemetry of any kind
- No crash-reporting SDKs
- No network requests other than those Apple's Game Center framework makes on your behalf
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No data sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
